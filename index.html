CALM Brain Journey™

Building the prototype for how Brainnovation develops, delivers, and scales Brain Journeys

Purpose: Introduce CALM as more than a single product. It is also the prototype for a repeatable Brain Journey methodology.

The overall narrative: “CALM is the first test case for a repeatable Brainnovation system for developing Brain Journeys.”

  1. We need a methodology.
  2. CALM is the first Journey we are building with that methodology.
  3. We build and test the essential experience first.
  4. The participant Journey generates standardized assets.
  5. Those assets activate the rest of Brainnovation.
  6. What we learn from CALM becomes the template for every Journey that follows.

Why This Approach?

Build the essential product first. Learn before we scale.

Core principles

  1. 1Build the essential product first
  2. 2Evaluate before adding complexity
  3. 3Use technology to enhance the Journey, not define it
  4. 4Introduce coaching once the core Journey is structured
  5. 5Use the Logic Model and PDSA for disciplined development
  6. 6Pilot before investing heavily in technology, content, staffing, or infrastructure

What the Participant Actually Experiences

1AssessUnderstand current challenges, goals, baseline, and priorities.
2UnderstandLearn what CALM means and how stress affects the brain and body.
3PracticeBuild skills through practices, challenges, reflection, audio, and experiences.
4IntegrateApply those skills to real life.
5MeasureSee what is changing.
6AdjustUse experience and data to determine what happens next.

This is where you deliberately shift from how we build it to what the participant does.

  4. 4Build cognitive flexibility
  5. 5Recover and restore
  6. 6Reduce mental clutter
